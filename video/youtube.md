## Descripción

A 3-minute pitch for Track 2 (drug repurposing) of "Rare Disease, Real Kid: MVA Hackathon 2026".

PROBAND01 has mosaic variegated aneuploidy type 1 (MVA1), caused by two faulty copies of BUB1B (identified in Track 1). One defect produces two problems: chromosome mis-segregation, which raises cancer risk, and premature cell aging. The gene itself cannot be drugged, so we searched downstream, scoring candidates against eleven independent lines of evidence.

Candidates, ranked by evidence in children:
1. Metformin (approved drug)
2. Nicotinamide riboside (dietary supplement, a form of vitamin B3)
3. Fisetin (dietary supplement; adult data only)

This is a research hypothesis for a supervised single-patient discussion between clinical genetics and paediatric oncology. It is not a proven treatment and not medical advice. Recommended tumour surveillance for all MVA types (kidney ultrasound every 3 months, birth to age 7) is not replaced by any of this.
